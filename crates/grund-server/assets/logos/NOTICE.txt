Product logo notices
Source and trademark policy review: 2026-10-17.

Kasper accepted the trademark risk on 2026-10-17 for the product logos below.
That decision is not a claim that the trademark owners granted permission,
endorsement, sponsorship or affiliation.

Each vendored SVG is the complete, unmodified upstream file. Geometry, colours,
styles, metadata, proportions and native whitespace are preserved. The sprite
embeds each complete file as an image document, scaled to fit without cropping.
No external logo CDN is contacted by the browser.

The sources.tsv table records the source, policy, decision and attribution.
Those rows generate the individual notices below and the attribution at the
bottom of pages displaying the corresponding logos. Redis and MinIO ownership
statements therefore also appear on the same page as their marks.
The table's layout column selects square or wide containment. Cards reserve the
same 72-pixel column for both: square art stays 44 pixels and left-aligned;
wide marks fill a centred 72-by-44-pixel slot without distortion or cropping.

whoami has no official mark and uses the neutral glyph. Docker remains neutral:
its key represents arbitrary Docker Hub images, not a Docker template app.
